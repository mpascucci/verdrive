## About

VerDrive shows what your trip costs right now.
It reads your speed and altitude from the phone's GPS and runs a simple physics model of your vehicle: how much power it needs, and how much fuel that power burns.
Estimates are rough: about ±20–30% with the quick settings, better with the advanced ones.
Everything stays on your phone.

## Data

- **WLTC speed cycle** (`wltc.js`): Commission Regulation (EU) 2017/1151, Annex XXI.

No third-party code, fonts or images: the icons and the seven-segment digits are drawn in the app.
